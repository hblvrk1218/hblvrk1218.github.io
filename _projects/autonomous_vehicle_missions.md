Developed an integrated **ROS2-based autonomous driving system** capable of performing multiple dynamic driving missions, including obstacle avoidance, traffic-light recognition, lane following, and rear parking.

The overall system was designed using a modular architecture, with perception, decision-making, and vehicle control implemented as independent ROS2 nodes.

## Key features

- **ROS2-based modular architecture** for multiple autonomous driving missions
- **YOLOv8m-based perception** for lanes, vehicles, and traffic lights
- **LiDAR sensing** for obstacle detection and environment perception
- **Stanley Method** for path tracking and steering control
- **FSM-based mission management** for multiple autonomous behaviors
- Integration of **obstacle avoidance, traffic-light recognition, and rear parking**
